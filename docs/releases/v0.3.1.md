# 網金游俠模擬器 v0.3.1 — 武學與兵器修正

Download **Youxia-0.3.1-Windows-x64.zip**, extract all files, and run **Youxia.exe**.

- Restores the original 搏擊格鬥 name and functional categories for the seventeen basic skills.
- Enforces matching weapons for external arts, including sword, blade, staff, spear, whip and brawling weapons. Incompatible weapons cannot add damage to another art.
- Separates external, internal and lightness slots. Sect virtues, medicine, formations and other utility skills cannot become ordinary attacks.
- Corrects 打狗棒法 to staff, 混天功 to internal, and 倚天屠龍筆法 to spear according to the official classification.
- Shows categories, weapon requirements and per-art self-study limits in the martial-arts UI; adds weapon removal and two practice weapons.
- Retains basic-skill caps and imported skill prerequisites; limits the simulator's 紫霞神功、峨嵋九陽功、九陰真經 instructor entries to the initial level.
- Preserves old save levels and proficiency while migrating skill names and invalid equipped arts.
- Uses one weapon-aware damage calculation for solo PvE, parties and PvP.

All multiplayer participants must update to v0.3.1 (protocol 3). Existing character saves remain readable. The world now contains 116 items; other content counts are unchanged.

See [martial rules and sources](https://github.com/chon9/Jinyong-Emulator/blob/v0.3.1/docs/MARTIAL_RULES.md) for the verified mappings and remaining adaptations. Original quest chains, absolute-skill tiers, nine-yin conversion and full historical combat formulas are not yet reproduced.

Validation: 2,376 checks passed across content, martial rules, social rules, BGM, UI/save migration and two-process multiplayer. Network checks cover host-side weapon mismatch rejection and independent inner-art selection. The Windows ZIP is also extracted and launch-tested before publication.
