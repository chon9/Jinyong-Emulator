# 網金游俠模擬器 v0.4.0 — 學藝與尋路

Download **Youxia-0.4.0-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the whole archive, and run **Youxia.exe**. Godot and Python are not required.

## Learn through proficiency

- Instruction spends one learning point for 悟性-based proficiency instead of granting one level outright.
- Ordinary self-study spends three learning points and energy after a complete five-second cycle; interruptions grant no credit.
- Checks attributes, gender, morality, skills, personal master, sect rank, lifetime combat experience and quest flags, with a sixteen-special-art limit.
- Restores per-level cubic training thresholds and individual difficulty values; preserves old within-level progress proportionally.
- Adds staged learning events with actual conversations, choices, item deductions, trials, outcomes and rewards. Source data contains 29 routes; unsupported special steps remain explicitly blocked.
- Shows local teachers and missing requirements prominently, plus separate external, internal, lightness and parry selections and output-force control.

## Go where you intended

- Search places, NPCs, martial arts and learning events; results identify the actual room and distance.
- Preview the next step, start host-controlled travel through adjacent rooms, and cancel at any time.
- Stops on arrival, cancels the old route when choosing a different destination, and blocks travel during combat or leader following.
- Labels simulator station shortcuts and generates connector roads deterministically.

## Combat and content

- Uses martial arts, corresponding basics, equipment multipliers, parry, dodge, move unlocks and resource costs in one solo/party/PvP calculation.
- Removes character-level attack/HP bonuses and level-up full heals; lifetime 實戰 is retained separately.
- Incorporates newer owner-supplied reference content: 270 connected locations, 292 NPCs, 174 items, 137 enemies, 11 sects, 124 arts and 71 commissions.
- Keeps classic UI, music, parties, trades, consensual duels and opt-in PK.

All players must use v0.4.0 (protocol 4). Existing saves retain learned arts and inventory, subject to the new learning gates; unfinished travel and study cycles reset on load.
