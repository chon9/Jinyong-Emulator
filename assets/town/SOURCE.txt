Selected owner-supplied Jinyong Heroes 5 resources, imported for this project at the owner's request.

Town: Map044 geometry, tileset 10047 and panorama 100047, reconstructed into qingxi.webp.
Only tile geometry, directional passage flags and image references were read from RPG Maker XP data.
No source event commands, scripts, dialogue, characters' identities, saves or private data were imported.
Qingxi's named landmarks, NPC roles, quests and interaction points are this project's own adaptation.
World image: Graphics/Pictures/allmap.png. Its existing geographic labels are retained.
The world map offers regional dialogue and encounters; only Qingxi currently has a walkable town scene.

Characters: selected clean huashandizi_battle, huashandizi_battle2, tianlongsword2, tianbattle,
gujianchenbattle, azhu, monk2, wudang, huatiegan, yangguo, oldmonkbattle sheets.
Original 200px animation cells are registered consistently and cropped into 160px atlas cells.
Pure magenta XP colour-key pixels become transparent. Frames with corrupt coloured stripes were rejected.
Character identities and martial builds in this game are original; artwork does not imply original-source identities.

Effects: selected 003-Attack01, 007-Weapon02, 013-Heal02, 023-Burst01 animation sheets.
Sound effects: 062-Swing01, 089-Attack01, 048-Explosion01, 105-Heal01, 055-Right01.
Fonts were inspected; the existing LXGW WenKai TC font and its OFL license are retained.
The existing five JY-Mini OL music tracks remain; see assets/bgm/SOURCE.txt.

This provenance record documents the selected source files; it does not grant ownership of third-party assets.
